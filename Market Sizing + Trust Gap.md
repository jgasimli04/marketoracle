# Market Sizing + Trust Gap

## Mind-map (nested)

- Agentic Commerce
  - Market Sizing (2030)
    - Global ceiling: $3T–$5T (agentic commerce projections)  [oai_citation:0‡McKinsey & Company](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-agentic-commerce-opportunity-how-ai-agents-are-ushering-in-a-new-era-for-consumers-and-merchants?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    - US B2C “orchestrated revenue”: up to ~$1T  [oai_citation:1‡McKinsey & Company](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-agentic-commerce-opportunity-how-ai-agents-are-ushering-in-a-new-era-for-consumers-and-merchants?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    - US agentic commerce (narrower definition): $300B–$500B (~15%–25% of US e-commerce)  [oai_citation:2‡Bain](https://www.bain.com/insights/2030-forecast-how-agentic-ai-will-reshape-us-retail-snap-chart/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    - US agentic shoppers spend: $190B–$385B (~10%–20% share)  [oai_citation:3‡Morgan Stanley](https://www.morganstanley.com/insights/articles/agentic-commerce-market-impact-outlook?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
  - Trust Gap (why adoption stalls at checkout)
    - Reality check:
      - 58% use AI to research
      - 37% start a purchase journey via AI
      - only 17% feel comfortable completing a purchase through AI  [oai_citation:4‡channelengine.com](https://www.channelengine.com/en/blog/state-of-marketplace-shopping-channelengine-report?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    - What “trust” means at the moment of purchase (the minimum)
      - Price truth (final total is consistent)
      - Stock truth (available now)
      - Delivery/returns truth (ETA + policy confidence)
      - Proof (reviews/specs/warranty)  [oai_citation:5‡channelengine.com](https://www.channelengine.com/en/blog/state-of-marketplace-shopping-channelengine-report?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
  - What this implies for Market Oracle (your wedge)
    - Don’t sell “more AI”
    - Sell “confidence infrastructure” that closes the trust gap:
      - normalize price/stock/delivery signals
      - generate “why this pick” + alternatives
      - package proof artifacts for agents + humans
