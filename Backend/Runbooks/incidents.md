## Purpose
Incident process and templates.

## Severity guide (starter)
- SEV0: data breach / widespread payment failure
- SEV1: checkout broken for majority of users
- SEV2: elevated errors, partial outage
- SEV3: minor degradation

## Incident template
- Summary
- Impact
- Timeline
- Root cause
- Mitigation
- Corrective actions
- Preventative actions
- Links to dashboards/logs/traces
