# Amazon - Rufus + Walled Garden

## The constraint
Amazon restricts external agent access. Optimization is listing-centric.

## Why it matters
> [!warning] Why
> A universal intelligence layer must handle “closed ecosystems” differently: infer signals without direct protocol integrations.

## Intelligence approach
- normalize what you can (pricing, listing quality, review density)
- treat as “opaque market” module
- avoid dependency on official APIs
